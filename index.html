Frequently asked questions

The important stuff, answered.

Why is a starting detail required?

Recurring maintenance works best when the vehicle begins from a reasonable clean baseline. The Complete Refresh establishes that condition.

Do I need a long-term contract?

No. Membership becomes month-to-month after the first prepaid month.

Do you bring your own water?

Yes. Never Dirty brings its own water, products, towels, wash media, vacuum, and equipment.

Do I have to remove all my belongings?

No. Customers are asked to remove valuables and important items. Loose accessible belongings may be placed in a provided bag and left on the passenger seat.

What happens when it rains?

Rain, severe weather, unsafe temperatures, or unsuitable ground conditions may require rescheduling.
