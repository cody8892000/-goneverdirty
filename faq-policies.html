General questions

Frequently asked questions

These answers cover the most common questions about Never Dirty memberships and one-time detailing.

What is Never Dirty?

Never Dirty is a premium mobile vehicle-maintenance and professional detailing service. The main goal is to keep vehicles consistently clean through recurring service instead of allowing them to become heavily soiled between occasional details.

Do you bring your own water and supplies?

Yes. Never Dirty brings its own water, professional detailing products, microfiber towels, tools, and routine service equipment unless a special arrangement is required.

Where do you provide service?

Primary coverage is in Saline County, Illinois. Select reasonably located customers in Williamson and Franklin Counties may also be accepted depending on routing, travel, service type, and availability.

Are memberships and one-time details scheduled on the same days?

Recurring membership service is primarily scheduled on weekdays. One-time Refresh and Signature details are primarily scheduled on weekends, although availability can vary.

Does requesting service guarantee an appointment?

No. Website forms are service requests. Never Dirty reviews the vehicle, condition, location, schedule, and service area before confirming a quote or appointment.

What products do you use?

Never Dirty primarily uses Adam's detailing products, premium microfiber, and professional vehicle-cleaning tools. Product selection may vary based on the material and service being performed.

Personal items

Belongings policy

Customers are requested to remove personal belongings before service, but the vehicle does not automatically have to be empty.

How loose belongings are handled

  • Customers are asked to remove valuables, cash, medication, weapons, sensitive documents, electronics, and fragile items.
  • Loose everyday belongings may be placed into a provided belongings bag.
  • The bag will normally be left on the passenger seat unless another arrangement is made.
  • Areas blocked by large, fragile, unsafe, or excessive belongings may not be fully cleaned.
  • Never Dirty is not responsible for items that were not disclosed, were already damaged, or were left unsecured in the vehicle.
Important: Customers do not have to cancel solely because they forgot to empty the vehicle. However, removing unnecessary belongings allows for a faster and more complete service.

Appointments

Scheduling and weather

Mobile detailing depends on safe access, usable space, and workable weather.

How is my appointment confirmed?

An appointment is confirmed only after Never Dirty approves the request, verifies availability, confirms pricing, and receives payment when required.

What happens if it rains?

Light weather does not always require cancellation, especially when covered space is available. Heavy rain, storms, high winds, unsafe lightning, freezing conditions, excessive heat, muddy access, or conditions that prevent proper work may require rescheduling.

What site conditions are required?
  • A paved or gravel driveway, suitable parking lot, or firm non-muddy grass area
  • Enough room to work safely around the vehicle
  • Reasonable runoff conditions
  • Permission from the property owner or employer when needed
  • Vehicle access as arranged before service
Will I receive a day-of message?

Yes. Customers may receive a text or other message confirming timing, vehicle availability, access, and any weather-related changes.

Financial policies

Payments, cancellations, and rescheduling

Clear payment and scheduling expectations protect both the customer and the service route.

Payment policy

  • Payment is due before service unless Never Dirty explicitly approves another arrangement.
  • Recurring membership customers may be required to pay by the stated weekly or monthly deadline to remain on the schedule.
  • A service request is not considered confirmed solely because a form was submitted.
  • Additional charges will be discussed before major extra work is performed whenever reasonably possible.

Cancellations and no-access situations

  • Customers should provide as much notice as possible when rescheduling.
  • If the vehicle is unavailable, locked, moved, unsafe, or inaccessible at the scheduled time, the appointment may be treated as a late cancellation.
  • Repeated cancellations, payment issues, or access problems may result in loss of recurring route placement.
  • Never Dirty may reschedule when weather, illness, equipment issues, emergencies, unsafe conditions, or route disruptions prevent proper service.

Quotes and labor

Vehicle condition and pricing

Advertised prices assume a vehicle is within the normal condition expected for that service level.

Conditions that may increase pricing

  • Heavy pet hair
  • Severe staining or embedded debris
  • Excessive trash or personal-item handling
  • Smoke, mildew, strong odors, or odor-treatment requests
  • Heavy mud, clay, coal dust, construction debris, or worksite buildup
  • Excessive bugs, sap, tar, road paint, oxidation, or bonded contamination
  • Large vehicles, lifted vehicles, oversized tires, or unusually complex interiors
  • Vehicles that require restoration-level labor rather than routine detailing
Never Dirty will make a reasonable effort to identify condition-based price changes before service. Photos and honest disclosures help prevent surprises.

Material safety

Pre-existing damage and service limitations

Detailing can improve cleanliness and appearance, but it cannot reverse every form of age, wear, damage, or neglect.

What counts as pre-existing damage?

Examples include failing clear coat, peeling paint, cracked leather, worn fabric, loose trim, brittle plastics, torn carpet, damaged switches, broken vents, leaking seals, chipped glass, rust, corrosion, scratches, dents, and previously repaired surfaces.

Can every stain or odor be removed?

No. Some stains, dye transfer, burns, permanent discoloration, deep contamination, smoke odor, biological contamination, and material damage may improve without being fully eliminated.

Can you refuse unsafe work?

Yes. Never Dirty may refuse or stop service involving needles, active pests, hazardous chemicals, weapons, mold, bodily fluids, unsafe structural conditions, aggressive animals, threatening behavior, or any situation that creates unreasonable risk.

Mobile service requirements

Vehicle access and service area

Customers are responsible for ensuring that the vehicle and service location are ready at the scheduled time.

  • The vehicle must be available at the approved location.
  • Interior service requires the vehicle to be unlocked, keys to be available, or the customer to be present.
  • Workplace service requires permission from the employer or property manager when applicable.
  • Street-side work, unsafe traffic areas, steep grades, muddy ground, or inadequate room may be declined.
  • Travel outside the normal route may include an additional fee or may not be accepted.

Documentation

Recording and marketing policy

Service visits may be documented to support safety, quality control, training, marketing, and dispute prevention.

  • Exterior and interior before-and-after condition may be photographed or recorded.
  • Recording may document pre-existing damage, vehicle condition, service completion, and access.
  • Customer names, addresses, license plates, private documents, and identifying information should not be intentionally published without appropriate permission.
  • Marketing use may be limited or declined when arranged in advance.
  • Never Dirty may still document condition privately for operational and dispute-prevention purposes.

Recurring service

Membership policies

Membership pricing is based on maintaining a vehicle that has already been brought to an acceptable baseline condition.

Why is a starting detail required?

The starting detail creates the clean baseline needed for recurring maintenance pricing. It prevents a heavily soiled vehicle from being treated as a routine maintenance visit.

What happens if I skip several visits?

If the vehicle falls outside normal maintenance condition because of missed visits, heavy use, weather, travel, or unusual contamination, extra labor or another Refresh Detail may be required before normal membership pricing resumes.

Can I add another vehicle?

Yes, subject to route capacity and approval. Additional approved vehicles receive 25% off eligible recurring service pricing.

Can my membership be ended?

Yes. Either party may end recurring service. Never Dirty may discontinue service because of nonpayment, repeated access problems, unsafe conditions, abusive behavior, route changes, excessive cancellations, or a vehicle repeatedly falling outside the agreed maintenance condition.

These policies are designed to keep scheduling fair, pricing accurate, and service quality consistent. Specific written quotes or agreements may add or modify terms for unusual jobs.
