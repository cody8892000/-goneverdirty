Condition and pricing policies

Final pricing depends on the work required.

Starting prices assume an average-condition machine. Any additional charge will be discussed before the additional work is performed.

  • Caked mud or clay: additional labor starts at $30.
  • Farm-use buildup: manure, feed, straw, grease or similar material may require additional pricing.
  • Hunting debris: blood, animal matter or biological contamination may require custom pricing or service may be declined.
  • Heavy grease: excessive oil, grease or mechanical residue may require additional degreasing charges.
  • Oxidation or faded plastics: cleaning does not guarantee complete restoration. Correction or restoration is priced separately.
  • Aftermarket equipment: speakers, lighting, electronics, winches, snorkels, windshields, roofs and accessories may affect pricing or cleaning limitations.
  • Mechanical condition: machines with exposed wiring, damaged seals, loose components or known electrical problems may be declined.
  • Personal belongings: customers should remove tools, weapons, valuables, coolers and loose equipment before service.
