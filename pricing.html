Vehicle condition and pricing

Some vehicles require additional time.

Standard package pricing assumes an average-condition vehicle. Final pricing may be adjusted before work begins when extensive labor or special treatment is required.

  • Excessive mud: starting at an additional $20.
  • Excessive trash or personal items: starting at an additional $20.
  • Heavy stains, embedded pet hair, or excessive sand: priced according to the labor and service required.
  • Biohazard contamination: quote required and service may be declined.
  • Mold or severe contamination: quote required and service may be declined.
  • Oversized or unusually configured vehicles: may require custom pricing.
