Frequently Asked Questions

Before your first appointment.

Why is a Full Detail required before membership?

We first bring the vehicle to our maintenance standard. Future visits are then focused on keeping it consistently clean instead of repeatedly restoring a heavily soiled vehicle.

Do I have to be home?

No. As long as we have access to the vehicle, permission to perform the service, and payment has been arranged, you do not need to be present.

What happens if it rains?

We will contact you if weather prevents us from safely providing a quality service. Your appointment will be rescheduled for the next reasonable opening.

How do I pay?

Payment is due before each scheduled appointment unless another arrangement has been approved.

Are memberships long-term contracts?

No. Memberships are month-to-month and may be canceled. Services already scheduled or completed remain payable.

What does the customer need to provide?

The vehicle must be parked in a safe and accessible service area with adequate runoff. Paved driveways, gravel driveways, or firm non-muddy grass areas may be acceptable. Never Dirty brings its own water and detailing supplies.
