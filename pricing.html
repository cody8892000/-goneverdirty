Vehicle categories

Vehicle size guide

Size is based on actual vehicle dimensions, interior capacity, configuration, and expected labor—not only the manufacturer’s label.

Small

Compact cars, coupes, small sedans, and similarly sized vehicles.

  • Compact sedans
  • Two-door coupes
  • Small hatchbacks
Medium

Most sedans, small crossovers, and standard two-row SUVs.

  • Full-size sedans
  • Compact crossovers
  • Two-row SUVs
Large

Three-row SUVs, minivans, crew-cab trucks, and larger passenger vehicles.

  • Three-row SUVs
  • Minivans
  • Crew-cab pickups
Oversized or specialty vehicles: Extra-large trucks, commercial vehicles, lifted vehicles, vans, heavily modified vehicles, and unusual configurations are quoted individually.
